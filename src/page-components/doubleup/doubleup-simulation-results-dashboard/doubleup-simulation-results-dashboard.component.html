Break Even Streaks

Using the true count to dictate when to take insurance is a fair approximation, but there are 2 factors that keep it from being exact.

To accurately know if taking insurance is the correct play, the player needs to know how many 10s remain in the shoe as well ad how many total cards are left in the show.

Aces and 10s are both counted in the same in many counting systems so the exact number of 10s is not accounted.

The number of non-counted cards, 7 - 9 in Hi Lo, are also assumed to come out in proportion to the rest of the cards, but this is not always the case.

Since this simulation has the exact number of cards remaining as well as the exact number of 10s remaining, the exact percentage of 10s remaining in the deck can be calculated and from that the exact EV for each instance is the simulation where insurance was offered.
